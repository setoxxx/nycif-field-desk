# Archived / Do-Not-Use WordPress Page Templates

Store old, hidden, cleanup, and draft-only page bodies here.

These pages should not be linked publicly unless they are rebuilt and moved to `wordpress/pages/active/`.

## Rules

- Keep archive pages as Draft or hidden in WordPress.
- Do not link from homepage, footer, menus, templates, or public hubs.
- Do not add newsletter shortcodes back.
- Do not reuse old homepage content.
- Do not reuse duplicate map embeds for `/map/`.
- Trash only after cleanup is fully stable and confirmed.

## Archive note guidance

Archive pages should make three things clear:

1. The page is old and should not be used publicly.
2. The current live replacement page or route should be named.
3. The page should stay draft or hidden until cleanup is complete.

Keep archive bodies simple. Avoid shortcodes, embeds, maps, newsletter output, prototype navigation, and old homepage sections.
